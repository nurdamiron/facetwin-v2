Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. FaceTwin is operated by [legal name, address] ("we"). Contact: hello@facetwin.app.

The short version

  • The face scan (face points, face shape, skin guide) runs on your device and is not uploaded.
  • When you ask for an edit (a retouch or a look) and only after you tick the consent box, the photo you chose is sent through our server to Google's Gemini AI, which makes the edit. We do not store that photo; the edited image comes back to your browser.
  • Photos, edits, saved looks and history are stored only in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • We receive what you type in (email, quiz answers) and your plan choice. We never receive your photos.
  • You can delete everything stored on your device from Settings → Privacy at any time.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address and your consent choice for emails.
  • Quiz answers: goal, looks you picked, skin concerns you chose, minutes per day, face-shape label.
  • Usage events: which screens you open and actions such as starting the quiz or opening the paywall, through the Meta Pixel, to measure our ads. Meta may combine this with data it holds about you; see Meta's privacy policy.
  • Marketing parameters: the ad campaign that brought you (UTM tags, fbclid).
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number.

Face data and photos

Face points and skin estimates are computed by an on-device model (Google MediaPipe) in your browser and stay on your device. When you request an edit, the photo, which shows your face, is sent to Google's Gemini API through our server to generate the result. We ask for your explicit consent before the first photo is sent, and you can say no. We do not keep the photo, we do not create or store a face template, we do not use the photo to identify you and we do not use it to train models. Google processes the photo under its Gemini API terms. If you are in Illinois, Texas, Washington or another place with biometric privacy laws, this consent is how we ask for your permission before the photo is processed. Why we use your data

  • To send your plan, reminders and product emails (consent; unsubscribe anytime).
  • To measure and improve our ads and onboarding (legitimate interest / consent where required).
  • To process payments and prevent fraud (contract).

Sharing

Google (Gemini API) to make the edits you request, hosting provider, email platform [name], analytics and ads (Meta), Stripe for payments. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

Photos sent for editing are processed in memory by our server and are not stored by us. Device data stays until you delete it or clear your browser. Email and quiz answers are kept while you have an account, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, objection and portability (GDPR/UK GDPR), and the right to know and delete (CCPA/CPRA). Write to hello@facetwin.app. You can complain to your local data authority (e.g. the ICO in the UK).

Children

FaceTwin is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

FaceTwin gives general face-care routines and style ideas. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. See a dermatologist for skin concerns.
